Experimental Class

Nobody reads the Terms and Conditions. Timothy read all 211 pages.

For three hundred years, the Wardells have been a family of mages. Forty-one orbs on the mantel prove it.

Timothy Wardell has studied for eleven years. He knows all 412 classes in the registry by heart. He is ready.

The System gives him one that isn’t in the registry.

[Experimentelle Klasse] (translation pending). Level 1 (beta). His fireball is a [Fireballe]. His shield comes with a warning in a language nobody can read. And instead of [Identify], he got [Developer Commentary]: he can read the notes the System’s developers left for each other.

He is legally forbidden to tell anyone what they say.

He knows, because he read the Terms and Conditions. All 211 pages. Nobody reads the Terms and Conditions.

Now the dungeon that feeds his city is coughing up boots, the bread has laces, and the notes say the dungeon is nearly full. Timothy can’t say a word about it until he can prove it the hard way, with sources. And in a month, he goes down for his trial.

A light, funny LitRPG about a very careful boy, a very janky System, and showing your work.
