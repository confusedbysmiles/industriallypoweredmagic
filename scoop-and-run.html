Scoop and Run

Her weapon is having read the paperwork. Theirs is having written it.

Meridian lost its war four days ago. The Vesperine Concord doesn’t so much conquer worlds as process them. Joining is called Reconciliation. Anyone who hasn’t joined is listed as outstanding, and outstanding items get closed.

Astrea is a black-ops medic. When a covert mission goes sideways and takes the chain of command with it, the first mate promotes her to fleet medic over the radio, believing he’s dying, and orders them to leave him behind. The audio is bad. It is the only record of her appointment. The fleet she’s now medic of no longer exists, and the Board that would confirm her has stopped answering.

So she does the reasonable thing and steals the empire’s command ship.

What she has left is the last running copy of the Board’s medical system — REGIS, which nobody calls REGIS — and a provider record that says PENDING. Every port, every contract and every friendly-looking form offers to fix that.
